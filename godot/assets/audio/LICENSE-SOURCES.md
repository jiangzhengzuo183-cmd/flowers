The Kenney download returned HTML, not an archive; no purported Kenney sound is included. Its response was retained only in the local verification directory.

Music is selected at low volume by scene group: bakery / night and school / consultation and lab / hospital (Vaporware). Shared recordings do not restart on dialogue changes. Master/music/effects/mute controls are local user preferences. Browsers start playback only after a user input. No sound is generated merely for a portrait expression change.
